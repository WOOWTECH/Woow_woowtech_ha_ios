woowtech aiot is the mobile app for woowtech smart-space hubs.
Once connected to a hub set up by you or your administrator, you can run the whole space from your phone — at home, in the office, in a shop or on a factory floor.

FEATURES
• Dashboards: view and control lights, switches, air conditioning, sensors and other devices on your hub
• Real-time notifications: your hub's automations can send alerts to your phone
• Phone status sharing: optionally share battery, network and other phone status with your hub for automations; each item can be turned off
• Home and away: set separate addresses for your local network and a secure external connection

BEFORE YOU START
• This app requires a woowtech hub that has already been set up; it does not work on its own
• Ask your hub administrator for your account and connection details
• Access from outside requires a secure external entry configured by your administrator

PRIVACY
Your data stays on your own hub; WOOW cannot access it.
Push notifications pass through WOOW's relay, which forwards them in real time and does not store their content.
Privacy policy: https://aiot.woowtech.io/privacy
